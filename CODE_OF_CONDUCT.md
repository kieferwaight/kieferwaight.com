# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Participants in this project are expected to make collaboration respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Use clear, good-faith communication.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, or sharing private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to [Kiefer Waight](mailto:kiefer.waight@icloud.com). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this code of conduct.
