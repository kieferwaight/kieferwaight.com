# Architecture advisory and fractional CTO support

When a system is too important to guess about and too busy to rebuild blindly, I provide a focused outside view. The goal is not a long report. The goal is a decision-ready understanding of the system, its risks, and the next practical move.

## Engagements

### 48-hour architecture and scaling audit

A short, evidence-led review for a team that needs clarity quickly. I inspect the system boundary, deployment path, data flows, operational dependencies, and the failure modes that could block the next stage of growth.

Typical outputs:

- Bottleneck and risk inventory
- Architecture boundary map
- Security and secret-custody observations
- Immediate mitigations and prioritized next steps

### AI integration roadmap

For teams moving from a model demo to a production system. The work focuses on routing, inference placement, context boundaries, observability, cost controls, and the operating model around the AI workflow.

Relevant public work includes [local-first multi-node AI](/writing/local-first-ai/) and [custodial secrets architecture](/writing/custodial-secrets-architecture/).

### Infrastructure modernization

For inherited or growing platforms where reliability depends on recovering context. I help define the current system, isolate the risky seams, and create a migration path that respects uptime, ownership, and the team's capacity.

See the [University of Texas Arlington transformation](/case-studies/uta-website-transformation/) and [inherited systems field guide](/writing/inherited-systems/).
