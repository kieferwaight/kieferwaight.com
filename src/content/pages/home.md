## Architecture judgment

I start with observation. Before changing a system, I want to understand what it does, who depends on it, where the uncertainty lives, and what evidence is available. I care about boundaries that hold under failure: private networks instead of exposed ports, volatile secrets instead of credentials at rest, declarative orchestration instead of tribal memory, and feedback loops instead of unexamined automation.

The standard is simple: leave the system easier to understand, easier to operate, and easier to improve than it was when I found it.
