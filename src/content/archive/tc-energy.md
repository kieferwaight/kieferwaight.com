# TC Energy

**Role:** Senior Angular Developer
**Dates:** December 2021 - August 2022

This entry is a personal account of the role, not a third-party or archived record. There is no external source or press citation to link to; it is included here for a complete career history.

## What the work involved

Built and maintained an Angular-based portal for managing data from pipeline meters. The engagement included an overhaul of AWS deployment and authentication using a multi-repository strategy, and a UI pass using Tailwind CSS to bring styling consistency across teams working on the portal.

## What this role demonstrates

- Enterprise portal development in a structured, compliance-conscious environment
- Deployment and authentication modernization
- Multi-repository operations with clear team and ownership boundaries
- Collaboration with non-technical decision-makers on UI direction
