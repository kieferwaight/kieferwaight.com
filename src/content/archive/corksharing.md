# CorkSharing

I built the CorkSharing platform, including its web and mobile applications and API. My involvement also covered setting up the offshore engineering team and traveling through Napa Valley to help establish the product.

The work combined software delivery, team building, and time in the market the product was intended to serve. It is part of my broader history of building marketplace products around travel and experiences.

## Platform, applications, and API

My technical responsibility spanned the platform and its customer-facing applications. I built the web and mobile apps together with the API, working across the interfaces people used and the services behind them.

That breadth is central to the project: my contribution covered the software across multiple application surfaces, with responsibility extending into the platform supporting them.

## Establishing the offshore team

I also set up the offshore engineering team. Building the product included establishing the team that would work on it, connecting technical delivery with the practical work of organizing a distributed development effort.

This makes CorkSharing an example of my work across both implementation and engineering team setup. The project required attention to the people doing the work as well as the applications being built.

## Product work in Napa Valley

I traveled through Napa Valley to help set up the product. That brought my involvement beyond remote software development and into the place the product served.

The field work belongs alongside the platform and team work in this account. My role crossed those responsibilities: building the applications, establishing an engineering team, and participating directly in product setup on the ground.
