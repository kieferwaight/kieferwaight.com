# AppealingStudio

The original about page described the studio that Kiefer Waight co-founded with Melanie Waight. It framed the company as a strategy-first web and mobile studio and established the background for the later GetMyBoat work.

## What the page established

### Studio identity

AppealingStudio positioned itself as a web and mobile development agency with a strategy-first posture.

### Partnership model

Melanie handled design while Kiefer handled the code, which matters because it explains how the public work was organized.

### Working style

The original page described a progression from concept to beta to a broader finished product.

## Timeline notes from the source

### 2009

The studio launches as a web and mobile development agency.

### 2010

Early marketplace work is described through ElectronicPenny.

### 2011

Product concepts such as PocketSpheres, PitchBrite, and MentionTribe appear in the studio history.

### 2012

ZigAir adds travel and charter-product context to the studio record.

### 2013

GetMyBoat becomes the biggest public example of the studio\'s marketplace work.

### 2014 to 2015

Later projects and events reinforce the studio\'s product, marketing, and launch history.
