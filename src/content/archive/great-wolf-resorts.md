# Great Wolf Resorts

**Role:** Development Consultant
**Dates:** July 2016 - December 2018

This entry is a personal account of the role, not a third-party or archived record. There is no external source or press citation to link to; it is included here for a complete career history.

## What the work involved

Built middleware connecting the Opera reservation system to the customer-facing website for rate calculations. The engagement also included integrating Salesforce Marketing Cloud for outbound marketing email transactions and developing a new gift card processing system for online bookings.

## What this role demonstrates

- Integration between a core reservation system and a web-facing booking experience
- Operational automation for outbound marketing and customer communications
- Transactional and loyalty workflow implementation in a hospitality context
