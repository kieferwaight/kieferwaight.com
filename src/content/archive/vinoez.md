I explored Napa Valley with the Vinoez team, moving through winery production spaces and the grounds around them. The photographs bring together details that are easy to consider separately: the equipment used on site, the space devoted to barrels and tanks, and the landscape a visitor encounters.

The sequence starts with the team beside production equipment, moves through storage and handling areas, then opens out to the vineyard and gardens. It closes with a box of wine: a small detail that brings the record back to the finished product. I have kept that progression because it gives the visit a context beyond individual snapshots.
